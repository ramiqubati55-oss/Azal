# AUHD Medical Research Evidence Register — 29 October 2026

## Scope and verified totals

This register is limited to **Anesthesia Technology, Midwifery, and Specialized Nursing**. It contains **19 source-linked records**: **14** entries in the university anesthesia repository, **3** graduation research titles documented by the university's 14 May 2025 news report, and **2** peer-reviewed journal articles with AUHD affiliations independently visible in publisher/PubMed records. Program assignment is 14 anesthesia, 2 midwifery and 3 nursing. Cross-disciplinary relevance is recorded separately and must not be counted as another unique work.

The registry is stored in `data/medical-research-2026.json`, with a searchable presentation on Arabic, English and Persian research pages.

## Evidence rules

1. A university repository record is **not** described as a journal publication unless an external journal/publisher citation confirms it.
2. A supervisor is not automatically an author. Author fields for university repository entries remain unassigned when the source does not identify students/authors.
3. The year shown for university repository entries is the year **listed by the repository**, not necessarily a journal publication year.
4. For journal articles, AUHD affiliation is confirmed from the publisher or PubMed article record. A publication with AUHD affiliation does not prove exclusive AUHD authorship.
5. News about a graduation research discussion confirms the announced project title and supervision, not an indexed journal publication.
