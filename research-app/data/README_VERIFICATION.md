# AUHD Research Index — Issue 01 (2026): source metadata and verification status

This package is a public BIBLIOGRAPHIC DISCOVERY INDEX compiled for Azal University for Human Development (AUHD), Sana'a, Yemen. It is **not** a certified list of 725 university-authored or peer-reviewed publications. Linked publications belong to their original authors and publishers. The catalogue does not establish affiliation on its own.

## Source and coverage
Source CSV: [AUHD_Research_Index_Issue_01_725_Records.csv](./AUHD_Research_Index_Issue_01_725_Records.csv)
Interactive browsing: https://ramiqubati55-oss.github.io/Azal/research-app/

The CSV includes **725 records** divided as:
- 544 book chapters
- 84 research articles
- 40 graduation projects
- 20 student reviews
- 10 review articles
- 8 conference papers
- 19 other document types (including corrections, preprints, theses and withdrawal notices)

There are 654 DOI URLs, but 724 records have empty author fields and author affiliation has **not been verified** for this dataset. The CSV contains both a 656-record core group (الأساسي) and a 69-record additional group (المضافة). Categories are copied from source; they must not be presented as evidence of journal peer review. A DOI link by itself does not prove AUHD authorship, publisher recognition or university membership.

## Responsible interpretation
1. Do not add these entries to institutional research totals, ranking submissions, verified-faculty records or approved research without title-level author and affiliation evidence.
2. A book chapter, student review or graduation project is not automatically a peer-reviewed research article.
3. An original publisher DOI or source link is a pointer for verification, not permission to repost copyrighted full text.
4. Distinguish cataloguing confirmation from author identity or publisher-affiliation verification.
5. For a verified institutional record, establish title, authors, year, publisher, persistent identifier and institutional affiliation using primary-source evidence; then link to the verification platform.
