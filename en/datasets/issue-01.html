Scope and rights

This is a bibliographic metadata dataset, not a repository of 725 full-text scholarly files. The index contains journal articles, book chapters, student projects and other record types. CC0 registered in DataCite covers the deposited dataset compilation, not permission to republish the underlying journal articles or book chapters.
