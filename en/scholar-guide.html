Improving Google Scholar Discovery

A checklist for the university's official repository. A link from GitHub alone does not make a paper eligible for Google Scholar.

  1. Provide one stable HTML page per scholarly work with its complete author-written abstract visible without sign-in.
  2. Use accurate citation_title, one citation_author per author, and citation_publication_date.
  3. Identify student projects, dissertations, technical reports and peer-reviewed journal articles accurately.
  4. Provide searchable, accessible full-text PDFs where permission allows, with a proper title, authors and bibliography.
  5. Only use citation_pdf_url for real eligible PDF files.
  6. Use simple HTML links for browsing by year and discipline; avoid JavaScript-only discovery.
  7. Ensure crawlers can access the official research pages without noindex or restrictive robots rules.
  8. Audit sample records for metadata correctness, HTTP availability and broken links.

Do not invent authors, publication dates, journal names or PDF URLs. Scholar indexing is not guaranteed and may take time.
