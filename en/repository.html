Rights-Safe Deposit Workflow

  1. Obtain author/rightsholder permission or document an applicable open license.
  2. Provide approved full text or an authorized accepted manuscript, with confidential personal or patient data removed.
  3. Document title, authors, subject, year, version, DOI, source URL and reuse license.
  4. Attach publisher affiliation evidence and institutional editorial approval before any new upload.
  5. Provide a persistent downloadable file and documented correction/withdrawal processes.

Operational guidance only; formal institutional approval of deposit policies is still required.
