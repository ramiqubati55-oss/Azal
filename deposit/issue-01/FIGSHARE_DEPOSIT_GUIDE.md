## Purpose
Prepare one **Dataset** or **linked metadata record** under an authorized free `figshare.com` individual account affiliated with AUHD. This is a **725-record bibliographic catalog** — *not* 725 journal articles or full texts.

## Submitted support request
A nonbinding technical inquiry was sent to `support@figshare.com` (copy `rankings@auhd.edu.ye`) on 13 November 2026 to confirm whether the safest route is a linked record referring to the existing Harvard DOI or a second public copy linked to the original.

## Required manual account workflow
1. Visit https://figshare.com/ and sign in through an authorized academic account. If no account exists, sign up on the free individual `figshare.com` service. Do not select a paid institutional SaaS offering.
2. Under **My data** choose **Create a new item**, then **Dataset**. For a metadata-only linked record, follow the documentation at https://help.figshare.com/article/how-to-upload-and-publish-your-research .
3. Check that the account does not already own a deposit of DOI `10.7910/DVN/WU0FVP` or a dataset with the same title.
4. Copy the approved metadata from `FIGSHARE_METADATA.json`. Choose **CC0 1.0 only for the bibliographic dataset**, consistent with DataCite's registration for the existing Harvard deposit. It does NOT grant permission to republish referenced copyrighted articles.
5. If making an actual secondary upload, attach only the original bibliographic CSV, normalized JSONL, and applicable technical documentation; exclude the underlying 725 publisher manuscripts/PDFs unless explicit individual reuse rights have been checked.
6. Add **the existing Harvard Dataverse DOI as a related item**, using the relation confirmed by Figshare Support. On figshare.com free individual accounts the platform typically creates its **own** DOI for newly published items. Do not overwrite it with Harvard's existing DOI, or claim both are a single DOI.
7. If unsure about what relation Figshare supports for a mirrored bibliographic dataset, keep the item private while awaiting support guidance. Do not publish a duplicate without clear provenance.
8. Once published, collect the **real Figshare item URL**, any assigned DOI, and a screenshot or API result, then update the university portal's international deposit status.

## Why this does not constitute institutional Figshare membership
The personal free plan allows publishing academic outputs with the university affiliation. A bespoke institutional Figshare repository or SaaS licence is a different, generally paid service. Neither account creation nor the act of submitting a dataset grants academic accreditation or ranking inclusion.
